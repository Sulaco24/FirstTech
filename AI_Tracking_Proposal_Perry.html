02 — The Problem

Why This Matters Now

🔴 No visibility on AI quality risk

AI-generated code goes to production without any labeling. If issues arise — regressions, bugs, security — there's no way to identify whether AI contributed to them or not. No accountability chain.

🟡 Blind investment in AI tooling

Claude licenses are being issued and expanded. Without usage data, there is no way to measure ROI — whether speed improved, whether quality held, or whether teams actually use the tools they're given.

🟣 No data for strategic decisions

As the account optimizes costs, data on AI-driven productivity gains becomes a key argument for maintaining or growing team size. Right now that argument cannot be made with numbers.

🔵 Industry direction: mandatory disclosure

Leading engineering organizations (Google, GitHub, Atlassian) are making AI contribution tracking standard. Clients in regulated industries are beginning to require disclosure of AI-generated code in deliverables.

03 — What We Propose

Three-Layer Tracking System in Jira

📊

1. AI Contribution Field

  • Custom Jira field: "AI Contribution (%)"
  • Range selector: 0–10 / 20–30 / 40–60 / 70–90 / 90–100
  • Required on ticket close for Stories, Tasks, Enhancements, Issues
  • Already prototyped and tested by Anton on MIGRADATA
  • Rollout to all streams with custom per-team screen configs
⚡

2. Speed & Quality Signals

  • Cycle time per ticket (created → done) correlated with AI % field
  • Bug rate per sprint — track bugs linked to AI-assisted stories
  • Reopen rate on AI-assisted tickets vs. manual
  • Sprint velocity delta over time (with/without AI adoption)
  • Automated Jira reports — no manual collection needed
✅

3. Utilization Verification

  • Team-level AI usage dashboard (per stream)
  • Monthly adoption rate — % tickets with AI contribution flagged
  • Trend tracking: is adoption growing or stalling?
  • Distribution heatmap: which streams lead, which lag
  • Input for license allocation decisions

Key Metrics Captured

⚡ Development Speed

  • Cycle time reduction (AI vs. non-AI)
  • Sprint throughput delta
  • Story points per engineer per sprint
  • Time-to-first-commit on complex tasks

🛡 Development Quality

  • Bug rate on AI-assisted stories
  • Reopen / rework rate
  • Hotfix frequency correlation
  • Regression rate in AI-heavy sprints

📈 AI Utilization

  • % tasks with AI contribution reported
  • Average AI contribution % by stream
  • Adoption growth month-over-month
  • License usage vs. actual engagement
